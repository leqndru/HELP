# DSSP Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 16, 2026

DSSP processes limited information to provide support services, including:

- Discord usernames and user IDs.
- Ticket messages and descriptions.
- Attachments and transcript files.

This information is used only for support purposes. Authorized staff may access ticket contents.

DSSP stores support role IDs in a local database. Ticket messages and attachments are handled through Discord, and transcripts may be stored separately.

For questions or data requests, contact the server administration.
